This directory is intentionally empty of artwork.

Cover images now come from two places:
  1. The live metadata providers (AniList / Jikan) serve their own CDN URLs
     for real titles. Nothing is copied into this repository.
  2. The bundled offline catalog renders generated SVG covers through
     `/api/poster` (see `lib/poster.ts`). They are deterministic, need no
     network, and carry no third-party licensing question.

The previous placeholder JPEGs were removed: they were collected from image
search with unverified licensing, and after the catalog rewrite nothing
referenced them any more.

If you add local artwork, put owned or properly licensed files here and point
a catalog record's `coverPoster` at `/posters/<file>`.
See `docs/PREVIEW-ASSETS.md` for optional hover-preview clips.
